First observations (draft)

  • 9 of 21 groups are virtual. In-person groups cluster on the coast and in the South Bay.
  • Likely gaps: no in-person group listed for inland North County (Escondido, San Marcos, Vista), the Ramona and Alpine backcountry, or El Cajon proper.
  • 9 groups meet on Wednesdays. Only one evening option (YOPD Care Partners, 6pm) and one Saturday option (quarterly), which is hard for working care partners.
  • Only one Spanish-language group (Chula Vista).
  • Website region tags are inconsistent: almost every group is also tagged "San Diego – City", and some in-person groups (Santee, Español) have no area tag.
